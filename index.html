GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Let’s clear a few things up.

How do I know if I need a repair or a new roof?+

Your roof’s age, the extent of damage, and its overall condition all matter. An inspection helps determine whether a targeted repair or a full replacement makes more sense.

What should I do after a storm?+

Stay safely on the ground, document visible damage, and arrange a professional roof assessment. If you suspect an active leak, take steps to protect belongings and contact a roofing professional promptly.

How long does a roof replacement take?+

Timing depends on your roof’s size, materials, weather, and any underlying repairs. Your project plan should include a timeline specific to your property.

Can you help me choose roofing materials?+

Yes. Tell us about your priorities, style, and budget so we can discuss suitable roofing options and explain their maintenance and performance considerations.
